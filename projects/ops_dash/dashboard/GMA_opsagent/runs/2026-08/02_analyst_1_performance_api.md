- **Interpretation:** **Fact —** Stronger absolute retail did not eliminate the relative-availability imbalance. ## Investigate Next
1. **Investigation — Reconcile universes first.** Document operational brand/model denominators, CloudTheory peer membership, EV inventory coverage, and Coupe mapping. Preserve supplied operational SOI/SOM until reconciliation; do not combine cross-file shares.
2. **Investigation — Test allocation productivity.** Obtain dealer locations, inventory age, in-transit status and trim-level retail for Southern/Eastern accumulation versus Mountain States, South Central and improving Western SUV performance.
3. **Investigation — Diagnose G70’s support/share contradiction.** Compare engine/trim availability, shopper funnel, offer eligibility, payment competitiveness and aged stock. Separate model-year and regional effects before considering incentive changes.
4. **Investigation — Separate price change from mix change.** Build a complete historical trim crosswalk and distinguish within-trim advertised-price movements from trim/nameplate weighting. Current inventory-weighted prices alone cannot identify discounting or customer demand mix.
5. **Investigation — Validate incentive response before prescribing spend.** Obtain realized transaction/lease outcomes, regional program exposure and a separate GV70 EV incentive measure. Require an appropriately controlled response model before recommending precise dollars, volumes or elasticities.
